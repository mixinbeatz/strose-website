The Saint Rose App
for Android

Mass times, events, the bulletin, prayer, and your parish family, on your Android phone or tablet.

The Android app is in its early-access stage on Google Play. Anyone in the parish can join. It takes about two minutes. Do these steps on your Android phone or tablet.

1
Join the tester group

Tap the button, then tap Join group. Use the same Google account that your phone's Play Store uses.

Join the group
2
Become a tester

Tap the button, then tap Become a tester.

Become a tester
3
Install the app

Tap the button to open the app in the Play Store, then tap Install. Sign in with Google, Apple, or email once it opens.

Open in Google Play

During early access the Play Store may show the app under a temporary name. That's expected.

Says "item not found"? Wait a few minutes after step 2 and try again. If it still doesn't appear, go to your phone's Settings → Apps → Google Play Store → Storage, tap Clear cache, then reopen the Play Store and search for Saint Rose.
Using a Saint Rose or other work email? Google Play only works with Google accounts. Use the Gmail or Google account that's signed in to your phone's Play Store. If you'd like to use your parish email instead, you can turn it into a Google account at accounts.google.com/signup (choose "use your existing email").
Please keep the app installed. Google requires a group of testers to use the app for two weeks before it can be listed publicly on Google Play. You'll get updates automatically.
Have an iPhone? The Saint Rose app is already available in the App Store. See saintroseanthem.org/app.

On a computer? Scan this with your Android phone's camera.

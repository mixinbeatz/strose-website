Mass & Sacraments

St. Rose celebrates all the sacraments. Whether you are preparing for one yourself, for your child, or are simply curious, the parish office is glad to help you find the next step.

Call Parish Office

Baptism

Welcomes a child or an adult into the Church and the life of grace. Call the parish office to begin preparation.

First Eucharist

Children prepare for their First Holy Communion through our Faith Formation program. Register for Faith Formation

Reconciliation (Confession)

The sacrament of God’s mercy and forgiveness. All are welcome. See the times below.

Confirmation

Completes the grace of Baptism and strengthens us with the Holy Spirit. Preparation is part of Faith Formation and Youth Ministry. Learn more

Matrimony

If you are engaged, please call the parish office well ahead of the date you have in mind so preparation can be arranged.

Anointing of the Sick

Brings strength and peace to those who are seriously ill, facing surgery, or growing frail with age. Call the parish office to request a visit.

OCIA for Adults

For adults who want to become Catholic or complete their sacraments of initiation. You are always welcome to ask questions first, with no commitment.

CONFESSION TIMES

Confession is also available by appointment. Call (623) 465-9740. For Mass times, see Mass Times.

Community Content Guidelines

Ministry chats and community boards are meant to bring our parish together. When you post, please:

  • Treat others with the same charity and respect you'd want in person.
  • Stay on topic for the ministry or group you're posting in.
  • Avoid harassment, hate speech, threats, or content that demeans any person or group.
  • Avoid spam, solicitation, or promotional content unrelated to parish life.
  • Avoid sharing anything explicit, illegal, or that violates someone else's privacy.

Transportation of Minor to / from Church Campus
The Catholic Diocese of Phoenix “Policy on Sexual Misconduct,” as it pertains to Diocesan personnel, states: “Field trips or other outings involving a minor in places and situations where no other responsible adults are present are to be avoided.” Whenever minors are transported to or from parish programs or events, two responsible adults should accompany them in the vehicle.

Due to the limited number of participants in the St. Rose Youth Program and the times at which events occur, it may not always be feasible to have two adults in every vehicle. Exceptions are allowed when the parish has made reasonable efforts to provide a second adult without success, and the parent or guardian has provided written consent below.
